Dice Support

Help with Dice for iPhone and iPad.

Contact the developer

For questions, problems, or suggestions, email Forrest Boyer.

forrestboyer02@gmail.com ↗

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help.

Restore skin purchases

  1. Make sure the App Store is signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open Dice and go to Settings.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases and follow any prompts from Apple.

Restoration recovers eligible skin purchases. Saved profiles, settings, and roll history are stored locally on your device.

Purchase help

A purchased skin is still locked

Connect to the internet and try Restore Purchases in Settings. If a purchase is awaiting approval, the skin unlocks after Apple confirms it. If the problem continues, contact the developer using the email above.

Prices or purchases are unavailable

Check your internet connection and try loading the price again. Product availability can depend on your App Store country or region. Contact the developer if the problem continues.

Billing questions and refunds

Apple processes App Store payments. For a refund request, follow Apple’s refund instructions.
