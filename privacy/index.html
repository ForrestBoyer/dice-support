Dice Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Dice is an app for iPhone and iPad developed by Forrest Boyer. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Dice, visit this support website, or contact the developer.

Data stored on your device

Dice stores your settings, dice selections, favorite skins, saved profiles, and roll history locally on your device. Roll history persists between app launches without an automatic roll-count retention limit; available device storage remains a practical limit. This information remains until the app’s local data is removed.

Dice also stores when local usage tracking began, your completed-roll count, the dates on which you completed rolls, your last roll date, and the date and app version of its last rating request. This local usage information helps decide when to request an App Store rating and when earned skins unlock.

The app does not send this stored information to the developer or operate a developer-hosted account or cloud service.

Dice does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Apple and in-app purchases

Optional skin purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Dice uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to obtain and verify purchase status, unlock skins, restore purchases, and update access when a purchase is refunded or revoked.

The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. Apple handles information associated with its purchases and services under the Apple Privacy Policy and your Apple privacy settings.

Automatic update checks

Dice automatically checks Apple’s public App Store listing for available updates when a storefront is available. The request sends Dice’s app identifier and your App Store storefront country to Apple. As with other internet connections, Apple receives the connection’s IP address. The check does not send your roll history, saved profiles, preferences, local usage records, or purchase records.

Dice stores the storefront country, the last successful check time, cached update information, and which update notices have been shown locally on your device. This helps avoid repeated requests and notices. Updates are optional, and a failed check does not prevent you from rolling dice. Apple handles information received by its services under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information you send for support

If you email reyob.company@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, your message, and any attachments or other information you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems.

When the in-app mail composer is available, it prepares a support or feedback draft with the app version and build, iOS or iPadOS version, and device model. You can edit or remove these details before choosing to send the email. Roll history and purchase records are not automatically attached.

Support correspondence is handled through email providers. It is retained as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. You can stop sending information at any time and contact the same address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of support information. Information may be retained where required by law.

The developer does not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. Service providers handling support information on the developer’s behalf are required to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub’s handling and retention of hosting information are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

These pages do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts. Opening an external link or sending an email uses the services and privacy practices of that destination or email provider.

Removing app data

To remove Dice’s local app data, delete the app using iOS or iPadOS Settings. Offloading an app preserves its documents and data. Copies may remain in device backups, depending on your backup settings; manage those copies through your Apple or device backup settings.

Apple manages purchase records separately. Deleting Dice does not delete your App Store purchase history. Questions about Apple-held information can be directed to Apple through its privacy services.

Policy updates and contact

This policy will be updated when Dice’s information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Forrest Boyer at reyob.company@gmail.com.
